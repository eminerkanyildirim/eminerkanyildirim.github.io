About

I'm an electronic engineer focused on electronics R&D, hardware design and embedded firmware. At Vestel's appliance R&D lab, I test tumble-dryer electronic control boards for functionality, performance and IEC 60335 safety compliance. I also prepare requirements and technical specifications, benchmark products, and evaluate components and suppliers.

My project work connects the circuit board to the code and the data it produces: a wearable ECG sensor with a custom PCB and BLE firmware, a solar-car telemetry application, and discrete-logic and robotics course projects. Earlier, I contributed to a university solar-race team's electronics and completed internships in embedded software and automotive CAN systems.

How I work

  • Start from the real constraint — power budget, timing, cost, compliance — not the tool.
  • Prototype early, measure, and let the data settle the argument.
  • Prefer the simplest design that works: fewer parts, fewer failure modes.
  • Write firmware and tooling that the next person can actually read.

Currently exploring

Alongside my engineering work, I'm building my Linux, networking and embedded-security knowledge, with an interest in how connected devices handle firmware and communication safely.
